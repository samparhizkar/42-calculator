Per-digit animation

Unchanged digits stay locked. Type 56 then 6 — the "56" holds still while only the new digit slides in from below. Feels like a precision mechanical counter.

Equals spring

Each digit springs independently, staggered left-to-right. The result bounces in with a slight overshoot, then a teal warmup flash confirms the answer.

Active operator

Pressed operator holds its glow until the next digit clears it. You always know the pending operation without reading the expression line.

Sonar on =

Teal ring expands from the = key outward across the keypad when you press equals. Words typewrite in after.

Breathing idle

Result pulses slowly when you stop typing. The app feels alive, not frozen.
